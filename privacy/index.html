Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-29. This page explains what information we collect when you call us or visit this website and how we use it.

What we collect

This website has no forms and does not ask you to type in personal details. When you call us, we may collect your phone number, your name, your address and details about the damage so that we can send a crew. Like any website, our servers and hosting provider receive technical data your browser sends, such as your IP address and browser type.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

How we use information

We use call information to respond to your request, schedule work, explain scope and price, improve our service and keep records of work we perform. We do not describe or promise outcomes with an insurer.

Sharing

We share information with the crews and professionals who perform your job and with service providers that support our phone and website. We may share information when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party content

This site loads fonts and embedded maps from Google. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them, and they have their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it by email at contact@alisoviejowaterdamagepros.com or by calling (949) 670-0939. California residents may have additional rights under state law, and we will respond to valid requests.

Retention and security

We keep job records for as long as needed to serve you and meet legal and insurance needs. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do. Questions can go to 32 Journey, Aliso Viejo, CA 92656 or contact@alisoviejowaterdamagepros.com.
