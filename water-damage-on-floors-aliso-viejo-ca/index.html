Water Damage on Floors in Aliso Viejo, CA

Floors take the worst of a water loss because water ends up on them. Whether a floor can be saved depends on two things, the material and the kind of water. Clean water from a supply line is very different from storm water or sewage. This guide goes floor by floor and gives a decision framework you can use before the crew arrives.

Start with the water category

The industry standard ANSI/IICRC S500 groups water into three categories. Category 1 comes from a clean source such as a supply line. Category 2 carries some contamination, such as washing machine or dishwasher discharge. Category 3 is grossly contaminated, such as sewage or rising floodwater.

Time also matters. Clean water that sits in a floor for days can be treated as a higher category. The longer the water stays, the fewer materials can be saved.

Hardwood and engineered wood

Solid hardwood has the best odds when the water is clean and the response is quick. Boards can cup, but they often settle as they dry. Engineered wood has a glued veneer over layers, and water can swell the edges so they do not return.

Do not sand or finish until readings are normal. Wet wood trapped under a new finish fails. A technician measures with a meter and compares against a dry reference.

Laminate and vinyl plank

Laminate has a fiberboard core that swells when wet. Swollen edges stay swollen, so planks are usually replaced. If the subfloor is wet beneath laminate, the whole section may come up so it can dry.

Vinyl plank is waterproof on top but not underneath. Water gets in at the seams and sits under the planks. Planks can sometimes be lifted, the subfloor dried and the planks reinstalled, while glued-down floors are usually replaced.

Tile and concrete slab

Tile and a concrete slab handle water well, but the layers under tile matter. A thin-set bed or a backer board can hold water, and grout lines are porous. After clean water, tile is usually kept and the slab is dried.

A slab holds water for a long time and releases it slowly. That moisture can move up into flooring placed on top, so readings on the slab matter before any new floor goes down. If the water was sewage, the slab and grout are cleaned and treated.

Carpet and pad

Carpet and pad are different decisions. Carpet fibers can be cleaned and dried after clean water, while the pad underneath is a sponge and is often replaced. After Category 2 or 3 water, both are usually discarded.

Carpet that stays wet starts to smell. If it has been wet for more than a day, ask for an assessment. Lifting the carpet along an edge shows what is happening underneath.

How wet floors get dried

Drying begins with extraction. Portable or truck-mounted units pull water out of carpet and off hard floors. After that, air movers push air across the surface, and dehumidifiers pull water out of the air.

Some floors need special methods. Mats can dry hardwood by pulling moisture through the boards. Small holes can let air reach the space under a floor or behind a baseboard. Equipment is checked each visit and moved to the wettest areas.

What to do in the first hours

  • Stop the source and shut off the water.
  • Remove loose items, rugs and furniture from the wet floor.
  • Take dated photos before anything is moved.
  • Keep people and pets out of any water that may be contaminated.
  • Call a crew and tell them the floor type and how long it has been wet.

If the water came from a storm or a backup, read about flood damage restoration before anyone walks through it. If the floor was dried and now needs to be rebuilt, water damage repair covers replacement flooring, trim and paint.

Cost follows the floor type, the area, the water category, the number of drying days and whether the subfloor must be replaced. Matching an existing floor across rooms can mean replacing more than the damaged section. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Common questions

Can wet hardwood floors be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and the response was quick. Dry the floor, wait for readings to match a dry reference and evaluate for cupping before sanding. Long exposure or contaminated water usually means replacement.

Is laminate flooring ruined by water?

Laminate with a fiberboard core usually swells at the edges and does not recover. Planks are replaced, and the subfloor is dried before the new floor goes down.

Can vinyl plank be dried in place?

Sometimes. Click-lock planks can be lifted, the subfloor dried and the planks reinstalled. Glued-down vinyl is usually replaced.

How do I know if carpet pad needs replacing?

Pad is a sponge and usually holds water and contaminants. After any Category 2 or 3 water it is replaced, and after clean water it is often replaced too.

Who do I ask in Aliso Viejo about my flood zone?

The City of Aliso Viejo Public Works department is the floodplain contact, at (949) 425-2533. You can also search the FEMA flood map for your address.
