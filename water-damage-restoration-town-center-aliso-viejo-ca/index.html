Where Town Center and Vantis are

The Aliso Viejo zoning code lists Vantis as Specific Plan SP-1, The Commons as SP-2 and a Town Center Commercial district. Local news has called Town Center the geographical heart of Aliso Viejo. We did not find an official boundary map, so this page uses the sourced addresses instead of street lines.

Shea Properties lists Vantis at 90 Vantis Drive as a mixed-use community with 435 apartments built in 2016, along with retail, office and hotel space. A housing advocacy page lists a proposed 362-home project at 26501 Aliso Creek Road. We could not confirm its status.

Why multi-unit buildings need a different response

The 2022 Housing Element says 39 percent of Aliso Viejo housing is multifamily. In a multi-unit building, water travels. A leak above can wet a ceiling below, and a drain line shared by several units can back up into the lowest one. The first call is to the property manager, who can shut off shared water, and the second is to us.

Renters and owners deal with different documents. A renter's policy, if you have one, is separate from the building owner's policy, and the lease says who handles repairs. Ask the property manager in writing what is being done, and photograph your own belongings and any visible damage.

Mixed-use and commercial spaces

Town Center combines housing with shops, offices and a hotel. Water damage in a suite follows different rules than in a home, including tenant space, shared ceilings and business closure concerns. Our commercial water damage page covers that side, and the same phone number reaches the dispatcher.

First steps in an apartment or condo

  • Stop the source

    Call the property manager first so shared valves can be closed, then close the valve at your own fixture if it is safe. Ask the manager to check the unit above and the units beside you.

  • Power safety

    Do not touch outlets or switches near wet walls. If a light fixture is filling with water, leave the room, and call 911 or the utility for any electrical or gas concern.

  • Photograph and protect

    Photograph the damage in your unit and note the time you found it. Tell the property manager the unit number, the floor and where the water seems to start.

  • Keep people out

    Keep children and pets away from drain or toilet water. Shared drain backups can carry contamination from other units.

If the water is sewage

Sewage collection in Aliso Viejo is handled through the South Orange County Wastewater Authority according to the city hazard plan. The authority lists an active spill line at (949) 234-5400. Stay out of the room, keep children and pets away and do not try to clean it with a household vacuum.

Insurance and documentation

In a building with several owners and tenants, more than one policy can be involved. Photos, moisture readings and an itemized scope help each party see the same facts. The California Department of Insurance Residential Claims Guide says to report damage to your insurer to start a claim, so call yours early and log every contact.
