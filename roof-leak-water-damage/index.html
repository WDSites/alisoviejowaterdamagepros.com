When a roof leak becomes an emergency

A drip into a bucket is a nuisance. A brown ring that spreads while you watch, a ceiling that sags or water running along a light fixture is an emergency. At that point the water is reaching insulation, drywall and framing faster than anything can hold it. Our 24/7 line is open on nights and weekends. Same-day response is offered when available.

We should be clear about our part. We dry the ceiling, attic and walls and clean up what the water touched. We do not repair roofs. A roofer finds the opening and closes it, and our drying work keeps the inside from staying wet while that happens.

  • Catch and redirect

    Set buckets or tubs under drips and lay towels around them. Move rugs, electronics and furniture out of the wet zone.

  • Mind the power

    Do not touch a light fixture or fan that has water on it. Switch off the circuit only if the panel is dry and easy to reach.

  • Keep clear of bulges

    Stay out from under a sagging or bulging ceiling. Wet drywall and insulation can drop all at once.

  • Photograph it

    Take dated photos of the stain, the attic side if you can see it safely, and any roof damage visible from the ground.

Rain, wind and roofs in Aliso Viejo

The City of Aliso Viejo Local Hazard Mitigation Plan, completed in June 2024, says the city gets about 13.2 inches of rain a year, with heavy rainfall between October and April. An older National Weather Service record for the former El Toro station shows about 12.4 inches a year, heaviest from January through March.

That is a dry climate by national standards. It also means a small flaw in a roof can sit unnoticed for months and then show itself in the first hard storm of the season. The same city plan notes that windstorms are common in Southern California during Santa Ana wind conditions, and strong gusts can lift or shift flashing and loose pieces.

Safety steps for the first hour

Water and electricity are the main danger. If water is dripping through a ceiling light, a smoke detector or an outlet, keep people away from it. Call your utility or 911 for any sparking, burning smell or buzzing from wiring.

  • Do not climb onto a wet roof. A fall is a far bigger loss than a wet ceiling.
  • Do not poke a bulging ceiling while standing under it. Wet drywall and insulation can drop all at once, and a crew can relieve it with protection in place.
  • Move people and pets out of the room below a sagging ceiling.
  • Open nearby doors and run a fan only if the wiring is dry and the room is safe.
  • Call a roofer for the opening and call us for the water inside.

How we dry a ceiling and attic

The crew starts with moisture meters on the ceiling, walls and floor, and a thermal camera to trace wet areas that look dry. Wet insulation comes out because it stays damp for a long time and can hold moisture against framing. Sagging or stained drywall is cut back to dry material so air can move through the cavity.

Air movers and dehumidifiers then run until readings return to normal. If water dripped onto flooring, extraction units pull it from carpet and pad first. We work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard as a reference for water damage drying, which helps keep the scope clear. Larger jobs may need structural drying across several rooms.

Skylights, sliders, windows and other entry points

Not every ceiling stain means a hole in the roof. Skylight curbs, window frames and sliding doors can let in wind-driven rain, and water can travel along a rafter or a pipe before it drops. A stain in one corner may start some feet away.

Tell the dispatcher about every wet spot, even small ones. Your roofer can test for the source once the weather clears.

What changes the cost of a roof leak cleanup

Several things set the price. They are the size of the wet area, how many ceiling and wall sections come out, the amount of wet insulation, whether flooring or contents were soaked, the drying days and how hard the attic is to reach. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Insurance, temporary repairs and contractors

A California Department of Insurance claims guide says to stop leaks at the source, report water damage right away and keep a log of calls. It also warns about contractors who push you to spend heavily on temporary repairs, and says to wait on large permanent repairs until the adjuster has visited. Policies treat sudden damage differently from slow wear, so ask your insurer.

The Contractors State License Board storm damage release advises paying no more than 10 percent down or $1,000, whichever is less, getting a written contract and collecting three bids. Its roofing contractor guide says a building permit is generally needed when structural work is involved.

Mistakes to avoid and what to ask

The most common mistake is fixing the stain and not the water behind it. Paint over a wet ceiling and the paint bubbles again, and mold can start in the cavity. Another is waiting for the weather to clear before calling, while insulation soaks for days.

The City of Aliso Viejo residential submittal checklist requires Class A roofing on additions and does not allow wood shake or shingles there. It says nothing about existing roofs, so ask your roofer, or ask the Building and Safety office at (949) 425-2540 whether your repair needs a permit.

Roof leak help in Aliso Viejo condos and homes

If you live in a condo or a community with an HOA, ask your property manager who is responsible for the roof and tell them in writing the same day. Water that drops into a unit from above may also reach the home below. We work with owners and tenants on the inside damage, and we serve Laguna Niguel and Lake Forest as well. Common questions

Is emergency roof leak water damage service available in Aliso Viejo at night or on weekends?

Yes. The 24/7 line takes calls at night, on weekends and on holidays. Tell the dispatcher which rooms are wet and whether the ceiling is sagging. Same-day response is offered when available.

Do you repair the roof?

No. We dry and clean the ceiling, attic, insulation and walls. A roofer finds and closes the opening. We recommend calling both, since the roof repair stops new water and our work removes the water already inside.

Should I pop a bulging ceiling to let the water out?

Do not poke it while standing under it. Wet drywall and insulation can fall suddenly. Move people away, keep the room clear and call us so a crew can handle it with protection in place.

Will my insurance cover a roof leak?

It depends on the policy and the cause. A California Department of Insurance guide describes sudden and accidental water damage as typically covered and wear and tear as usually not. Report the damage promptly and ask your insurer.

Can the ceiling stain just be painted over?

Not until it is dry all the way through. Painting over a wet ceiling traps moisture in the drywall and insulation. We check with a moisture meter first, and then repair work can begin.
