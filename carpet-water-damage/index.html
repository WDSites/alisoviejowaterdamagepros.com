Carpet Water Damage in Aliso Viejo, CA

Why wet carpet is more than a surface problem

Carpet is three layers: the face fiber, the backing and the pad below it. Water passes through the first two and settles in the pad, which acts like a sponge against the subfloor. The top can feel nearly dry while the pad stays wet for days.

Wet carpet shows up in Aliso Viejo for ordinary reasons. The National Weather Service record for the former El Toro station averages 12.4 inches of rain a year, with the heaviest months from January through March, and that is when water can reach carpet at sliders, garage doors and entry thresholds. Leaks from water heaters, washing machines and upstairs bathrooms cause the rest.

Clean water or dirty water

The type of water decides the plan. Category 1 water comes from a clean source, such as a supply line. Category 2 water, such as a washing machine overflow or storm runoff, carries some contamination. Category 3 water includes sewage and flood water. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the usual reference for those categories.

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention advises throwing away carpeting that cannot be cleaned or dried quickly when sewage or flood water was involved. We follow the same logic. Clean-water carpet is often worth drying. Carpet that sat in sewage usually is not, and our sewage backup cleanup page explains that case.

First-hour safety steps

Stop the water before anything else. The California Department of Insurance claims guide says to stop a leak at the source right away, and warns that delay in cleanup can result in the growth of mold. Then keep your feet out of the wet area, since water and electrical cords do not mix.

  • Close the valve for the fixture, appliance or main line.
  • Turn off power to the room only from a dry panel and without standing in water.
  • Lift furniture legs onto foil or plastic, or move small items out of the room.
  • Take photos of the wet areas, then call us.
  • Keep children and pets off wet carpet if the water was not clean.

Our process

The Environmental Protection Agency guide to mold in commercial buildings lists three steps for wet carpet and backing that should dry within 24 to 48 hours. Remove water with an extraction vacuum, reduce humidity with a dehumidifier and speed drying with fans. We use the same order at home for a good reason.

Extraction comes before fans because fans on a soaked carpet only move water around and raise the humidity in the room. Taking the bulk water out first lets the dehumidifier work on moisture that is left.

Pad, carpet and floating the carpet

The pad is usually replaced, because it holds water, is hard to dry in place and costs little compared with the carpet. The carpet itself can often be dried. To reach the pad, we may release the carpet from the tack strip along one wall, lift it and prop it on air movers so air passes underneath. This is sometimes called floating or lifting the carpet.

After drying, the carpet is stretched back and re-installed over new pad. We check the subfloor and tack strip while the carpet is up. Wet tack strip can stain or rot and the subfloor may need more drying time. If the backing has delaminated, which means the layers have separated, the carpet will not lie flat and is replaced.

Stairs, bedrooms, rugs and stains

Stairs are hard to dry because carpet is tacked tightly to each tread and riser. Bedrooms often have thick pad. Area rugs, especially wool, are taken out for cleaning and drying away from the floor. Dye bleed from the rug can stain what is underneath, so move rugs early. Stains, odor and color change are separate from moisture, and we tell you what we see.

We cannot promise any carpet will be saved. The EPA notes that carpet may have to be thrown away if it becomes moldy, and a wet carpet that sat past the 24 to 48 hour window has a lower chance. We explain what the meter shows before anything is cut.

Renters, owners and who decides

The Census Bureau lists 19,421 households in Aliso Viejo, with 56.6 percent owner-occupied and a median gross rent of $2,774. That means many carpets belong to a landlord. If you rent, tell the property manager at once, in writing, and ask who approves work. Owners in a condo should also tell the association if water crossed a wall or ceiling.

What changes the cost

The water category, the square footage, whether the pad and carpet are replaced, the number of drying days, access to stairs and the amount of furniture to move all affect the price. A lightly wet carpet with clean water is a smaller job than a soaked room with a long wait. We explain scope and price before work starts.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Running fans over a soaked carpet before extraction.
  • Leaving old pad under new carpet.
  • Waiting a week to see if it dries on its own.
  • Keeping carpet that was soaked in sewage or flood water.

Common questions

Do you handle wet carpet at night or on weekends in Aliso Viejo?

Yes. The dispatch line is open 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call, say where the water came from and which rooms are wet. We can tell you what to shut off. Same-day response is offered when available.

Can my wet carpet be saved?

Often it can if the water was clean and drying starts quickly, but we cannot promise it. We check the carpet, backing and pad with a moisture meter. Carpet that sat in sewage or flood water is usually thrown away.

Do you replace the pad or dry it?

The pad is usually replaced because it holds water and is hard to dry in place. The carpet is often lifted, dried and stretched back over new pad. The meter readings decide in each room.

Why do you use extraction before fans?

Fans on a soaked carpet mainly move water around and raise humidity. Extraction removes most of the liquid first, so air movers and the dehumidifier only deal with what is left.

Will wet carpet grow mold?

It can if it stays wet. The EPA advises drying water-damaged items within 24 to 48 hours, and carpet may have to be thrown away if it becomes moldy. Call quickly and keep the room ventilated.
